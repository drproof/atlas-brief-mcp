# Inventory

Slot-based, 12 slots, stacks up to 20. No weight system.

## Crafting

Recipes consist of two to four ingredients. Failed crafting loses half the
ingredients, not all of them.

## Cold and clothing

Clothing takes no slot but uses a separate equipment area with three layers.
Each layer adds a warmth value.
